### 🥇 #1 1752vc Pitch Deck Analyzer — 95 votes · 43 comments

**Tagline:** Know what investors will say before you ever hit send  
**Website:** [1752.ai](https://1752.ai)  
**PH Link:** [producthunt.com/posts/1752vc-pitch-deck-analyzer](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/1752vc-pitch-deck-analyzer)  
**Hunter:** 1752vc Team  
**Makers:** Ben Kahan (1752vc, Santa Monica, CA)  
**Tags:** AI · Startup Tools · Pitch Deck · Free

1752vc Pitch Deck Analyzer is a multi-modal AI tool that delivers institutional-quality pitch deck feedback to founders in under 60 seconds. Built on a proprietary dataset of 25,000+ real pitch decks and tens of thousands of hours from due diligence calls and investor meetings, it evaluates not just text but also slide design, data visualizations, and narrative structure. The tool covers five core dimensions: problem framing, market sizing, competitive positioning, financial projections, and founder narrative — producing actionable, specific recommendations rather than generic writing scores. Developed by 1752vc, a Southern California investment and growth acceleration firm, it is completely free to use at 1752.ai.

### 🥈 #2 Neo — 93 votes · 21 comments

**Tagline:** A novel-writing tool from the author of Silo  
**Website:** [github.com/hughhowey/neo](https://github.com/hughhowey/neo)  
**PH Link:** [producthunt.com/products/neo](https://www.producthunt.com/products/neo)  
**Makers:** Hugh Howey (author of Wool / Silo series)  
**Tags:** Writing · Open Source · Productivity · Free

Neo is a distraction-free novel-writing application created by bestselling author Hugh Howey, whose Silo trilogy was adapted by Apple TV+. Built after nine years of searching for the right writing tool, Neo features auto-numbered chapters that renumber when new chapters are inserted, a "Darlings" folder for preserving deleted passages, "The Silo" full-screen focus mode, daily and project word goals, multi-format export (EPUB 3, Word, PDF, Markdown), and timestamped SHA-256-signed PDF backups. Available free for macOS, Windows, and Linux under the MIT license with all data stored locally — no accounts, no subscriptions, no telemetry.

**✅ Highlight:** Author name recognition + MIT open source creates a trust signal most productivity tools can't buy; Silo's global fan base provides instant viral distribution without any marketing spend.  
**⚠️ Risk:** Scrivener and iA Writer have deep roots in the serious novelist market; Electron-based cross-platform apps face skepticism from power users on performance grounds.

### 🥉 #3 Any Command — 91 votes · 14 comments

**Tagline:** Turn your phone into a second screen and trackpad for PC  
**Website:** [anycommand.io](https://anycommand.io)  
**PH Link:** [producthunt.com/posts/any-command](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/any-command)  
**Makers:** Polyhistor (Sopron, Hungary)  
**Tags:** Productivity · Remote Control · Android · Windows

Any Command transforms Android smartphones into comprehensive Windows 10/11 controllers over local WiFi, with zero cloud dependency. Beyond basic mouse/keyboard control, it adds real-time screen streaming, bidirectional file transfer, clipboard sync, virtual gamepad with motion controls, and — most distinctively — no-code workflow recording that lets users automate repetitive PC tasks by simply recording their actions once and replaying them. The app operates entirely on the local network with PIN authentication, positioning it as a privacy-first alternative to cloud-dependent tools like TeamViewer or Chrome Remote Desktop. Free tier covers basics; premium unlocks automation, screen viewing, and advanced features.

**✅ Highlight:** Local-first architecture plus no-code workflow automation punches above its weight against cloud-dependent incumbents on both privacy and offline reliability.  
**⚠️ Risk:** Android-exclusive limits the addressable market; 3.7-star Play Store rating suggests onboarding or reliability friction; mature alternatives (Unified Remote, TeamViewer) have larger ecosystems.

### 4️⃣ #4 Mossy — 90 votes · 12 comments

**Tagline:** A desk plant that wilts when you need a break  
**Website:** [heymossy.com](https://heymossy.com)  
**PH Link:** [producthunt.com/products/mossy-2](https://www.producthunt.com/products/mossy-2)  
**Makers:** @heymossy (developer + architect couple)  
**Tags:** macOS · Health & Fitness · Productivity · Free

Mossy is a native Swift macOS menubar app (1.4 MB, macOS 12+) that places a small plant in your menubar — the longer you sit continuously, the more it wilts, creating a gentle visual cue to take a break. Unlike standard break-reminder apps that just show a timer, Mossy uses AI to generate personalized break tasks based on a single sentence you provide at setup (e.g., "stretch my bad back," "teach me Spanish"). Configurable intervals (30 min / 1h / 2h), never blocks or dims the screen, requires no account, no subscription, and collects no data. Built with Claude Code and Cloudflare Workers; completely free.

**✅ Highlight:** Emotional design through the wilting plant metaphor creates stickiness that pure-timer apps lack; AI-personalized breaks address the "I don't know what to do with 5 minutes" problem that kills break habit formation.  
**⚠️ Risk:** macOS-only cap on total market size; crowded break-reminder space (Time Out, Stretchly, Lungo); no obvious monetization path for a free, no-account app.

### 5️⃣ #5 Lubb — 79 votes · 1 comment

**Tagline:** A realistic heartbeat for falling asleep  
**Website:** [thelubb.com](https://thelubb.com)  
**PH Link:** [producthunt.com/posts/lubb](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/lubb)  
**Makers:** Independent developer (@otasimaz)  
**Tags:** Sleep · Health · iOS · Haptics

Lubb uses the iPhone's Taptic Engine to produce rhythmic haptic pulses — simulating the experience of falling asleep on someone's chest — when you place the device under your pillow. Adjustable BPM (40–90, default 55), customizable haptic strength, optional audio accompaniment, and a "Drift" function that gradually slows the beat to zero over 25 minutes. Integrates with iOS Sleep Focus for hands-free activation. True-black display minimizes battery drain during the night. No sleep tracking, no accounts, no data collected. Free for up to 10 minutes; $9.99/year or $29.99 lifetime.

**✅ Highlight:** Occupies a genuinely novel haptic sleep-aid niche with no direct smartphone-based competitor — the "simulated human presence" angle resonates with people who sleep better with a partner nearby.  
**⚠️ Risk:** Tightly coupled to iOS Taptic Engine hardware limits platform expansion; user habit formation for a new sleep ritual is slow; $9.99/year monetization ceiling is low for investor-backed growth.

## 📈 Trend Summary

1. **AI startup tools are moving from efficiency to decision-grade intelligence.** 1752vc's analyzer — trained on 25,000 real decks and actual funding outcomes — represents a leap from "write better" tools to "know what investors will think" tools. The combination of proprietary vertical data + free distribution + VC brand-building is becoming the standard template for next-generation startup SaaS.

2. **Creator-backed open source is the new cold-start playbook.** Hugh Howey's Neo proves that an established creator releasing a free, open-source tool can out-market paid alternatives overnight — the name is the acquisition channel. Any Command similarly uses a local-first, privacy-first narrative to differentiate from cloud incumbents on story rather than features alone. In a saturated tool landscape, identity and philosophy are becoming product moats.

3. **Health and wellness tools are evolving from data dashboards to emotional design.** Mossy's wilting plant and Lubb's haptic heartbeat both bet on sensory experience and emotional resonance over metrics and tracking. The Saturday leaderboard placement (typically lower-traffic) shows these products are reaching beyond the early-adopter developer audience and resonating with general consumers — a sign of genuine mainstream potential.
