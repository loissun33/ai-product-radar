## 🔍 Product Analysis

### 🥇 #1 akta.pro — 401 votes · 66 comments

**Tagline:** "Private company data and signals API for the agent economy"
**Website:** [akta.pro](https://akta.pro/) | **Makers:** Siddhant Masson, Saswat Nanda, Neeraj Kanoi
**Tags:** API · Developer Tools · Artificial Intelligence · Lead Generation

akta.pro is a private company intelligence platform offering 20M+ companies with 70+ data fields, delivering 2x the coverage of PitchBook and 4x the depth of ZoomInfo/Apollo. Built for the agent economy, it provides real-time monitoring across 100+ event signal types — funding rounds, executive changes, expansion signals — with deduplication and impact scoring baked in. Accessible via API, MCP, or CLI, enabling AI agents and GTM professionals to pull actionable company intelligence without building scrapers or maintaining data pipelines.

#### Six-Dimension Deep Dive

| Dimension | Analysis |
|-----------|----------|
| 🎯 Positioning | The infrastructure layer for AI-native GTM: private company data + event signals + agent-ready API — positioned between scrapers (fragile) and enterprise data giants (expensive and slow) |
| ⚙️ Core Features | 20M+ company profiles with 70+ fields · 100+ real-time event signal types with deduplication and impact scoring · API, MCP, and CLI access · Consumption-based pricing |
| 💡 Tech Highlight | MCP-native access means AI coding agents and chatbots can query company intelligence directly — no dashboard required. This positions akta.pro as the default "company lookup" tool in AI-native workflows |
| 👥 Target Market | AI agent builders, investment teams running automated deal flow, and GTM professionals using AI SDRs or AI-powered CRM enrichment; any team replacing manual research with agentic pipelines |
| 📈 Growth Signal | 401 upvotes and top-of-day finish signal strong resonance with the developer and VC community. Consumption-based pricing lowers the trial friction that kills data-API adoption |
| ⚠️ Risk | Data freshness and coverage are the product — any gap in company coverage erodes trust quickly; competition from Crunchbase, PitchBook, and Apollo who are all adding API/agent access; pricing can spiral for high-volume agent use cases |

### 🥈 #2 Diet Claude — 347 votes · 377 comments

**Tagline:** "Never get blindsided by Claude's usage limits again"
**Website:** [dietclaude.com](https://www.dietclaude.com/) | **Maker:** Surbhi Singla | **Hunter:** Akhil BVS
**Tags:** Chrome Extensions · Productivity · Artificial Intelligence

Diet Claude is a Chrome extension solving one of the most frustrating developer experiences: hitting Claude's usage cap mid-session with no warning. It adds real-time session consumption tracking, remaining capacity, and reset timing directly to the browser. Beyond visibility, it includes token optimization through context refinement, prompt improvement suggestions, and model recommendations. When limits are reached, it transfers the entire conversation and context to an alternative LLM rather than forcing a manual restart.

- **Highlight:** 377 comments — the second most-discussed product of the day — reflects how universally painful Claude's usage limits are. The seamless LLM handoff (preserving full context) is the standout technical achievement; every Claude power user has experienced losing work at a cap wall.
- **Risk:** Dependent entirely on Anthropic's product decisions — if Claude extends limits, adds native usage visibility, or changes its API, the core value proposition shrinks overnight. Expansion into multi-model orchestration tools is the logical hedge.

### 🥉 #3 Agnost AI — 254 votes · 1,110 comments

**Tagline:** "Catch agent failures your evals miss"
**Website:** [agnost.ai](https://agnost.ai/) | **Makers:** Shubham Palriwala, Parth Ajmera | **Backed by:** Y Combinator
**Tags:** AI Metrics · LLM Developer Tools · Observability · Analytics · Artificial Intelligence

Agnost AI analyzes production conversations between users and AI agents to identify the failures that offline evals systematically miss: silent failures, behavioral drift, hallucinations, user frustration signals, hidden feature requests, and churn indicators. The platform groups issues into recurring patterns, links each insight directly to the exact users and conversations behind it, and provides tooling to create evaluations and ship fixes. Integration is 3 lines of code or OpenTelemetry. Currently processing over one million messages daily with a 20% launch discount and free tier available.

- **Highlight:** YC backing + 1,110 comments (most-discussed product of the day) confirms this addresses a critical gap in AI agent deployment. Offline evals only test what you know to test — Agnost catches the unknown unknowns in production. The "pattern grouping + conversation drill-down" UX is what turns raw logs into actionable fixes.
- **Risk:** Competes with growing observability players (Langfuse, Helicone, Arize) and LLM platform native analytics; agent market consolidation could commoditize monitoring; privacy concerns around passing production conversations to a third-party service.

### 4️⃣ #4 Jotform AI Data Assistant — 218 votes · 153 comments

**Tagline:** "Turn form data into insights and action with AI"
**Website:** [jotform.com](https://jotform.com/) | **Hunter/Founder:** Aytekin Tank (@aytekintank)
**Tags:** Productivity · Artificial Intelligence · Data & Analytics

Jotform AI Data Assistant converts form submission data into actionable insights through natural language conversation. Built directly into Jotform Tables and Inbox, it enables users to query data, generate charts, identify trends, create summaries, and perform bulk record updates — all without formulas, filters, or manual sorting. AI Columns further extend capabilities with automated data enrichment on submission entry.

- **Highlight:** Jotform's founder-led launch signals that this is a priority feature, not a bolt-on. The "query your own form data in plain language" experience removes the analytics bottleneck for the non-technical SMB segment — Jotform's core audience — who've never had easy access to data analysis tools.
- **Risk:** Limited defensibility as a feature inside a larger form builder; competing against native AI analytics in Google Forms, Typeform, and emerging AI-first form tools; power users may prefer dedicated BI tools like Metabase for complex analysis.

### 5️⃣ #5 Nimbia — 160 votes · 512 comments

**Tagline:** "AI screen-sharing calls for user onboarding"
**Website:** [nimbia.ai](https://nimbia.ai/) | **Maker:** Joris Machielse
**Tags:** AI Voice Agents · Customer Success · SaaS · Artificial Intelligence

Nimbia is an AI platform that conducts screen-sharing calls to onboard and train new software users. The AI speaks, listens, and can click on the user's screen — replacing human-led onboarding calls with a scalable, always-available AI equivalent. The first production deployment showed 40% faster growth, 1.4X higher week-1 activation rates, and improved trial-to-paid conversion compared to the prior solution.

- **Highlight:** 512 comments despite 160 votes signals this sparked genuine debate — the concept of an AI that talks, listens, and controls your screen during onboarding is provocative and technically impressive. The 1.4X activation improvement from a real production deployment gives it credibility beyond a prototype.
- **Risk:** High trust barrier: users must allow screen sharing and audio with an AI; concerns about data privacy during onboarding of enterprise software; UX must be extremely polished to avoid frustrating new users who are already in a learning curve.

## 📈 Trend Summary

1. **The "agentic infrastructure" layer is consolidating:** Three of today's top five (akta.pro, Agnost AI, and Nimbia) are infrastructure tools for AI-native workflows — company data APIs, agent monitoring, and AI-driven onboarding. The pattern is clear: as AI agents move into production, the tooling layer that supports them is maturing and catching strong market demand.

2. **LLM usage visibility is a genuine product category:** Diet Claude's #2 finish with 347 votes and 377 comments proves that managing AI model consumption limits is a real pain point worth building a product around. As multi-model workflows grow, tools that track, optimize, and bridge across model limits will expand beyond power users to mainstream AI-first teams.

3. **Production AI agent observability is the new APM:** Agnost AI's top-3 finish (with YC backing and 1,110 comments) confirms that "what's actually failing in my deployed agent?" is one of the most pressing unsolved problems in applied AI. Expect this category to grow rapidly as enterprises scale from PoC to production deployments.
