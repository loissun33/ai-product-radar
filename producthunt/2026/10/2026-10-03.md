## 📈 Trend Summary

**1. The AI agent infrastructure battle is moving to the "delivery layer"**

Today's #1, ZooWork (285 votes), positions itself as an "AI agent delivery platform" — its core thesis is that the bottleneck is no longer model capability but the ability to translate domain expertise into production-deployable agents. This marks a clear shift in AI competition from "who has the best model" to "who can actually execute in real business environments." Meanwhile, #6 Cubicle (112 votes, "a read-only live office for your AI agents") and #4 Muse Gadgets (152 votes, Meta's open-source hardware SDK) extend the agent ecosystem from software observability and physical hardware respectively — signaling that AI infrastructure is being contested across every dimension simultaneously.

**2. Messaging as a distribution channel: iMessage as the new AI interface frontier**

#2 miso.com (211 votes) demonstrates that embedding AI services inside existing communication habits (iMessage) beats forcing users to download yet another app — $2M+ in beta bookings proves the model works. The same logic appears at #10 Kilo (66 votes, "your agentic fitness coach in iMessage"). The value of messaging platforms as AI distribution channels is being systematically re-rated by the market, and this trend will accelerate as Apple expands its developer ecosystem access.

**3. AEO replaces SEO: brand AI visibility becomes table-stakes for enterprise**

#3 Prefer (165 votes) represents a pivotal trend inflection: as user queries migrate from Google to AI engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity, traditional SEO rankings are no longer sufficient — brands must actively manage how AI systems narrate and cite them. Prefer's "AEO execution layer" positioning, validated by enterprise customers including Twilio and Segment, confirms that real budget exists for this problem today. Expect explosive competitive entry into this category over the next 12 months.
