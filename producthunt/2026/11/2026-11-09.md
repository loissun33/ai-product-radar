## 📈 Trend Summary

1. **MCP is becoming the default integration protocol for AI-native products.** Product Hunt MCP (#1, 437 votes) and ProtoMap (#5, 166 votes) both expose their core value as MCP servers — PH for community discovery, ProtoMap for Protobuf dependency graphs. The pattern is clear: in 2026, the question is no longer "does it have a web UI" but "can my AI agent call it." MCP is the new API.

2. **The "AI visibility" arms race is here.** Dageno AI 2.0 (#2, 370 votes, top commenter turnout) targets the growing dread that AI assistants recommend your competitors by default. Arcjet AI security models (#4, 240 votes) address the flip side — protecting your own AI agents from being manipulated by adversarial prompts. Both products frame AI channels as contested territory that must be won and defended, a category that barely existed 18 months ago.

3. **Developer toolchains are going agentic, not just AI-assisted.** Databox CLI (#3, 297 votes) ships 10 agent skills alongside 91 commands, and ProtoMap (#5) serves its dependency graph over MCP so coding agents can query it in real time without re-scanning the codebase. Four of today's top five products directly address Agentic development workflows — the transition from "AI suggests code" to "AI builds systems" is driving a full retooling of the dev stack.
