### 🥈 #2 myAIcademy — 489 votes · 97 comments

**Tagline:** Learn AI skills for your specific role and team  
**Website:** [myaicademy.com](https://www.myaicademy.com/)  
**Makers:** myAIcademy Team  
**Tags:** Education · AI · Enterprise

myAIcademy is an enterprise AI fluency platform that generates individualized learning pathways based on each employee's specific role, tools, and objectives. It features an admin dashboard for tracking progress, and content refreshes automatically every 72 hours to stay current with the rapidly evolving AI landscape. Available on iOS and Android.

**🌟 Highlight:** Role-customized learning paths + 72-hour automatic content refresh ensure training is always grounded in real work context — not generic AI theory.

**⚠️ Risk:** Long enterprise sales cycles; strong competition from Coursera, LinkedIn Learning, and established LMS platforms with larger content libraries.

### 🥉 #3 Chalked for Mac — 398 votes · 72 comments

**Tagline:** Your replies ready with your work's full context  
**Website:** [usechalked.com](https://usechalked.com/)  
**Makers:** Chalked Team  
**Tags:** Mac · AI · Productivity

Chalked is a privacy-first Mac native AI assistant that generates contextually relevant message replies based on conversation history, your calendar, and current work context. Press Tab to insert a suggestion or hold fn to modify via dictation. It tracks commitments with source attribution and status monitoring. All processing happens on-device — no data leaves your Mac. Requires macOS 26 + Apple Intelligence. Free Beta for Apple Silicon Macs.

**🌟 Highlight:** On-device privacy + Apple Intelligence — context-aware reply drafting with zero cloud upload, built for agency operators, consultants, and multi-stakeholder founders.

**⚠️ Risk:** Limited to macOS 26 + Apple Silicon, constraining the addressable user base to a relatively recent hardware cohort.

### 4️⃣ #4 Snitch — 329 votes · 58 comments

**Tagline:** Your Slack org chart, built by everyone in it  
**Website:** [snitchforslack.com](https://snitchforslack.com/)  
**Makers:** Snitch Team  
**Tags:** Slack · HR Tech · Team Management

Snitch is a Slack bot that builds and maintains organizational charts by asking one simple direct-message question: "Who do you report to?" It detects structural issues like missing managers, reporting loops, and departed supervisors — and surfaces them proactively. Admins can manually correct any entry. No HR system integration required. Pricing starts at $19/month for up to 50 people, with a 45-day free trial.

**🌟 Highlight:** Crowdsourced org-chart creation that self-maintains as teams change — no HRIS integration, no IT project, no consultants.

**⚠️ Risk:** Limited utility for small teams; employees may resist disclosing reporting relationships; privacy-sensitive in some geographies.

### 5️⃣ #5 Clockwork — 267 votes · 44 comments

**Tagline:** The calendar where your AI agents show up for work  
**Website:** [clockwork (beta)](https://clockwork.vmoksh-shah179.workers.dev/)  
**Makers:** Vmoksh Shah (indie developer)  
**Tags:** AI Agents · Productivity · Developer Tools

Clockwork is a macOS app that brings calendar-based visual scheduling to recurring AI agent tasks. Instead of scattered cron scripts and CI logs, users book agent jobs on a month grid, select from multiple AI engines (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Hermes Agent), and run them in isolated OS-sandboxed git worktrees with hard budget bounds. Every run produces a searchable, auditable report. Ships with ready-made specialists like Dependency Surgeon and Test Doctor. Free during Beta with BYOK (bring your own API keys).

**🌟 Highlight:** An indie developer shipped a polished, multi-provider AI agent scheduler with sandboxed execution — filling a genuine workflow gap that enterprise tools haven't addressed yet.

**⚠️ Risk:** macOS-only; indie-built early Beta with uncertain long-term maintenance; BYOK model requires technical sophistication from users.
