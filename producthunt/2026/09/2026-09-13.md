## 🔍 Product Analysis

### 🥇 #1 Perplexity Hybrid Compute 1 — 133 votes · 2 comments

**Tagline:** Routes sensitive tasks to your laptop, frontier work to the cloud  
**Website:** [perplexity.ai](https://www.perplexity.ai)  
**Hunter / Maker:** Perplexity Team (Jon Staff, Mac Product Lead)  
**PH:** [producthunt.com/posts/perplexity-hybrid-compute-1](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/perplexity-hybrid-compute-1)

Perplexity Hybrid Compute 1 is a privacy-first hybrid AI system for Mac that automatically routes prompts between local on-device models and cloud frontier models. The system's built-in PII-Tracer classifier (0.6B parameters, 0.965 recall on long conversations) detects sensitive content and keeps it local — no manual configuration required. On-device models include Gemma E4B and Perplexity-tuned Qwen 35B variants; cloud inference falls back to Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol. Features real-time CPU/GPU/memory and token-rate monitoring, iPhone task queuing, and an open-sourced PII-Tracer for enterprise compliance auditing. Requires Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 15, and 32GB+ unified memory. Available for Pro, Max, and enterprise subscribers.

#### Six-Dimension Deep Dive

| Dimension | Analysis |
|-----------|----------|
| **Positioning** | Hybrid AI compute platform focused on automatic local/cloud routing — privacy as a product differentiator, not a checkbox |
| **Core Features** | PII-Tracer intelligent routing · Local Gemma/Qwen models · Cloud Opus 5 / GPT-5.6 fallback · iPhone task queue · Real-time resource monitoring |
| **Tech Highlight** | 0.6B-parameter PII-Tracer classifier open-sourced; enterprise IT teams can tune cloud/local thresholds for compliance; no upload of flagged content |
| **Target Market** | Privacy-conscious professionals, legal/medical teams, enterprise IT compliance, Apple Silicon power users |
| **Growth Signal** | AI privacy regulation tightening globally; local inference hardware costs falling rapidly; enterprise demand for data sovereignty accelerating |
| **Risk** | 32GB+ Apple Silicon requirement limits addressable market; Apple could ship native privacy-routing in a future macOS update |

### 🥈 #2 Resurf — 131 votes · 4 comments

**Tagline:** Capture things worth resurfacing  
**Website:** [resurf.so](https://resurf.so)  
**Maker:** Resurf Team  
**PH:** [producthunt.com/posts/resurf-2](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/resurf-2)

Resurf is a private visual knowledge library exclusively for Mac. Users rapidly capture links, notes, images, and media into a private inbox via shortcuts or drag-and-drop, then organize them into spaces. Content is browsed as visual cards with full-text search and filtering, a native PDF viewer, and a distraction-free reader mode. All data remains locally stored with no cloud uploads, no account required, and full offline functionality. Priced as a one-time purchase ($29–$49), rejecting the subscription model that dominates its category.

**Highlight:** Visual card browsing + one-time purchase + fully local storage — three values that resonate powerfully with users fatigued by cloud-first, subscription-heavy tools.

**Risk:** Crowded category (Raindrop, Readwise Reader, etc.); AI summaries and transcriptions are still "Coming Soon" — a gap competitors may close first.

### 🥉 #3 Cognition's SWE-2 — 121 votes · 2 comments

**Tagline:** Frontier-grade coding AI at 64% less cost  
**Website:** [cognition.ai](https://cognition.ai)  
**Maker:** Cognition (creators of Devin)  
**PH:** [producthunt.com/posts/swe-2](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/swe-2)

SWE-2 is Cognition's new coding model, post-trained from Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 (2.8T parameters). It scores 50.0% on the FrontierCode 1.1 Main benchmark — matching frontier-class rivals at 64% lower cost than Anthropic's Fable 5.1. Three effort levels (medium / high / max) let teams optimize cost vs. quality: the medium setting uses 81% less compute and 58% fewer turns than SWE-1.7. Reaches first code edit in a median of 18 steps (vs. 48 for the prior version). Available exclusively through Devin product surfaces (Desktop, CLI, Web, Fusion) under the $20/month Pro plan.

**Highlight:** Cost-performance breakthrough — frontier-level coding intelligence at a third of rival pricing, reshaping the AI coding market's expectations.

**Risk:** Locked to Devin's ecosystem with no standalone API; teams wanting infrastructure-level access to the model must adopt the full Devin stack.

### 4️⃣ #4 ScreenCursor — 112 votes · 3 comments

**Tagline:** Screen recorder with auto zoom effects  
**Website:** [screencursor.app](https://www.screencursor.app)  
**Hunter / Maker:** Phoenix  
**PH:** [producthunt.com/posts/screencursor](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/screencursor)

ScreenCursor is a Chrome extension that automatically adds zoom effects based on cursor movements and clicks during screen recordings. Finished videos require no editing. Works cross-platform (Windows, Mac, Linux), processes everything locally, requires no account, and ships as a one-time $49 purchase (50% off at $24.50 for the Product Hunt community during launch week).

**Highlight:** Cursor-driven auto zoom removes the entire post-production step for tutorial creators — a real time-saver for the technical screencasting audience.

**Risk:** Single-feature utility with a low ceiling; established players (Loom, CleanShot) are actively iterating on similar capabilities.

### 5️⃣ #5 Clipwise — 101 votes · 3 comments

**Tagline:** Create & publish content every day on autopilot  
**Website:** [clipwise.ai](https://www.clipwise.ai)  
**Maker:** Clienta Centra Technologies (Gurugram, India)  
**PH:** [producthunt.com/posts/clipwise](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/clipwise)

Clipwise is a fully agentic AI short-video platform that creates, renders, and auto-publishes videos to YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. Users choose from 100+ proven high-performing formats, connect their channels, and the platform handles scripting, visuals, voiceovers, music, and publishing schedule automatically. Supports major AI models (Seedance, Veo, Gemini, Kling, Flux) and outputs 4K quality video. Chat-based editing lets users adjust voice, pace, and style in natural language. Claims 1M+ creators and a 4.9-star rating.

**Highlight:** True "content autopilot" — from format selection to multi-platform publishing, the entire content pipeline is AI-managed.

**Risk:** AI-generated video content faces homogenization and platform copyright risks; brand differentiation at scale is difficult when every creator uses the same formats.

## 📈 Trend Summary

1. **Privacy × Local AI is the new battleground:** Two of Sunday's top products — Perplexity Hybrid Compute 1 and Resurf — both win on privacy-first, local-storage value propositions. Users in 2026 are actively demanding data sovereignty. Expect this to become table stakes rather than a differentiator in the next 12 months.

2. **Cost-performance disruption reshapes AI coding pricing:** Cognition's SWE-2 at 64% lower cost than frontier rivals signals a structural shift. As capable-enough models become cheaper, the market for AI coding tools will bifurcate: premium full-context agents vs. cost-optimized workhorses. Mid-market teams will default to the latter.

3. **One-time purchase pricing is staging a comeback:** Two of the five top products (Resurf at $29–$49, ScreenCursor at $49) explicitly reject subscription pricing — and their comment sections show strong community approval. In a world of subscription fatigue, buy-once pricing is increasingly a growth strategy for focused, single-purpose tools.
