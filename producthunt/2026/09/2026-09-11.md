## 🔍 Product Analysis

### 🥇 #1 Anysite.io — 219 votes · 57 comments

**Tagline**: Web Data Infrastructure for the AI Agent Era  
**Website**: [anysite.io](https://anysite.io)  
**Makers**: Andrew Kulikov (CEO), Dmitry Kononov (CTO)  
**Hunter**: Anysite Team  
**Tags**: AI · Developer Tools · APIs

Anysite.io is a web data infrastructure platform that converts any website into machine-readable, queryable data for AI agents and developers. Founded in 2024, the company positions itself as the "Stripe for web data" — a fundamental primitive that makes the open web programmable for AI applications.

The platform features a self-healing extraction engine that automatically adapts to website structural changes, eliminating the constant maintenance burden that plagues traditional scrapers. Access is provided via REST API, MCP (Model Context Protocol) Server, and CLI, with database integrations for SQLite, PostgreSQL, and ClickHouse. Supported sources include Instagram, Reddit, Twitter/X, YouTube, and SEC EDGAR. Pricing follows a credit-based pay-per-request model with a strict no-data-retention architecture.

### 🥈 #2 Loqua — 210 votes · 58 comments

**Tagline**: Context-aware voice typing that understands what you mean, not just what you say.  
**Website**: [theloqua.ai](https://www.theloqua.ai)  
**Makers**: Loqua AI Team  
**Tags**: Productivity · AI · Voice

Loqua is context-aware AI voice typing software for Mac and Windows. It transcribes speech at 220 words per minute — roughly 5× the speed of typing — while automatically removing filler words, restructuring sentences, and applying formatting (lists, headings, hierarchy). It integrates with 50+ apps including Google Docs, Slack, Notion, VS Code, and Gmail, and supports translation into nearly 100 languages. Loqua also features a screenshot-to-ask function and voice-controlled app automation.

**Highlights**: 220 wpm · 50+ app integrations · ~100 language translation · Voice commands · Screenshot analysis  
**Risk**: OpenAI Whisper and Apple's native dictation are improving rapidly; macOS Sequoia and beyond will likely integrate AI voice input natively, compressing the competitive window

## 📈 Trend Summary

1. **AI Infrastructure Layer Rising**: Both Anysite.io (#1) and Cline Desktop App (#5) are foundational infrastructure plays — one for the data access layer, one for the code execution layer of AI agent workflows. Product Hunt's audience is increasingly developer-heavy, and purely consumer-facing AI tools need stronger community mobilization to break the top 3.

2. **Voice as the New Interface**: Loqua's #2 finish with the highest comment count (58) on the day signals strong genuine demand around voice-AI interaction. As model latency continues to fall and accuracy improves, the "speak instead of type" workflow is transitioning from niche productivity hack to mainstream workflow. The middleware opportunity in voice input may close within 12–18 months as OS-level integrations accelerate.

3. **The v2 Renaissance — Established Products × AI**: Raycast 2.0 (#4) demonstrates the compounding flywheel of "existing product × deep AI integration." Loyal early users become natural amplifiers when a trusted tool ships a major AI-native upgrade. For products that already have genuine PMF, the winning move in 2026 isn't building a new AI wrapper — it's weaving AI deeply into the existing user journey.
